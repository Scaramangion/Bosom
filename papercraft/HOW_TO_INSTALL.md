# Put Papercraft on your phone's home screen

Papercraft is one page that works with no connection once it has been opened. Nothing you make leaves your phone.

## iPhone (Safari)
1. Open **https://scaramangion.github.io/Bosom/papercraft/** in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with the arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open the new **Papercraft** icon once while online: that keeps a copy for offline use.

## Android (Chrome)
1. Open the same address in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **⋮**, then **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**), then **Install**.

## Good to know
- It opens full screen, like any app, with Sudashorn as its icon.
- **Save** in the app opens the share sheet: **Save to Files**, AirDrop, Messages.
- Every time a new build is pushed to GitHub, the app picks it up the next time you open it with a connection. With no connection you get the last one you opened.
- The address is public: anyone with it can use Papercraft.
