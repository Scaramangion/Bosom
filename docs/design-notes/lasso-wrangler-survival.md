Instead of relying purely on firearms, the lasso becomes your primary survival tool—a mechanics-heavy, tactile device that connects combat, creature wrangling, head-swapping, and farm management into one cohesive identity.

Key Systems Built Around the Lasso
1. Creature Wrangling & The Farm Corral
Rather than simply killing nocturnal entities on night patrol, you can wrangle and drag them back to Kanan’s estate:
	•	The Monster Pen: Dragged entities can be tied off at posts near the cottage.
	•	Spore / Crop Mutation: Living entities tied near your farm plots passively mutate procedural plant growth (e.g., a bound Void-Moth turns nearby standard wheat into Luminous Spore-Grain).
	•	High-Value Railway Exports: Shipping live wrangled entities on the train yields far higher market returns than standard grain, accelerating the town's population growth slider.
2. Mechanical Head-Swapping via Lasso
When a nocturnal creature is bound by the snare:
	•	Head Extraction: You can reel yourself in close to decapitate or unseat the entity's head, snapping it onto Koto’s neck on the fly.
	•	Stat Stealing: Swapping heads mid-wrangle instantly changes your sight and movement properties in the middle of a fight.
3. Environmental Lasso Traversal
	•	Pulling Machinery & Levers: Use the lasso to pull distant train switches, unlatch cellars scanned by Claude, or rip down rotten timber barricades.
	•	Vertical Grappling: Latch onto high roof beams or tree perches to escape swarms during night patrols, setting up drop-down ambush attacks.
Sprite Sheet Additions: Wrangler Module
To support this expanded focus, we add 6 core wrangler sprite sets to the asset pipeline:
	1.	Koto Wrangler Poses (8-Way): Boot-planted leanback stance, two-handed rope reel, and shoulder-drag animation while pulling bound entities.
	2.	Rope State Overlays: Stretched tension lines, coiled snare rings, and tied-post knot graphics for the farm pens.
	3.	Entity Bound States: "Ensnared/Struggling" animation frames for nocturnal creatures and outcasts.
	4.	Heavy Duty Saddles / Leashes: Inventory grid items for upgraded lasso types (e.g., Iron-Core Lasso for heavy entities, Spore-Silk Line for silent catching).
This gives the game a distinct Gothic-Cowboy Survival flavor that makes wrangling feel just as essential as shooting or managing your inventory grid.
