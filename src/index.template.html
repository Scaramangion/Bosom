Was small for a boy his age. But compared to the architecture of the foreign land he was a dwarf; a grasshopper, a single moving iota of a thing in a landscape built for something quite larger than him.

He ran alone across a bridge that seems to stretch farther than reason should allow — a narrow spine of pale stone laid over nothing, carved everywhere with spirals and old geometric scars, patterns worn soft by centuries no one bothered to count. Around him, the world falls away into a canyon vast enough to swallow him whole: cliffs the color of dried bone, patches of stubborn green clinging where they can, and far below, a wide basin of sand catching what little light slips through the clouds. The sky sits low and heavy, grey-green and overcast, the kind of light that flattens everything beneath it.

For moons the boy dreamt of a princess with hair of molten gold, curled up in fear as a shower of shadows came upon her.

Lightning crashes in the sky over the never-ending bridge that leads to that great and terrible land of RAHJAI.

He has inherited his grandmother’s farm… and so his adventure begins.

An Age of Mystery Has Fallen Upon The Planet.

Across the desert lands, the balance between LIGHT and DARK has begun to waver.

Throughout the ages, whenever that balance is disturbed, the Supreme Spirit sends down a dew drop of its power to a human worthy. In this age, that human is a child.

That child is YOU!
